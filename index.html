XJTalk

AI-Powered Interactive Room for XJTLU EAP Group Discussions

01. Motivation & Research

The Why & The Gap

Addressing high anxiety and "brain blanks" in EAP students. Unlike Doubao or Padlet, XJTalk provides adaptive scaffolding and XJTLU-specific feedback.


The Stakeholders

Primary: EAP Students (low confidence, high anxiety).
Secondary: EAP Teachers (need data-driven tracking).
