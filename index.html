XJTAIK: AI-Powered EAP Discussion Room

A design journey from research to reflection, creating a low-pressure practice space to reduce anxiety and build confidence for XJTLU EAP students.

01 Research

Motivation & Research

The Why (200-word statement)

We chose the EAP student support track because our initial research revealed a critical pain point: most students experience high anxiety and "brain blanks" during English discussions, which prevents them from practicing before class and building confidence. Existing tools fail to address both the psychological safety and real-time feedback needs of multilingual learners. XJTAIK was created to bridge this gap by providing a low-pressure, AI-powered practice space that simulates real discussions while offering personalized, non-judgmental support.

The Gap (4 Papers + 4 Commercial Products)

Academic Papers - Strengths:

  • Validated the link between language anxiety and unwillingness to communicate
  • Low-stakes practice environments reduce anxiety
  • AI-driven scaffolding supports collaborative learning

Academic Papers - Missed Opportunities:

  • No real-time, non-intrusive support during discussions
  • Lack of EAP-specific turn-by-turn feedback
  • No combination of psychological safety and data tracking

The Stakeholders & Personas

Primary Users: EAP Students
Anxious students who avoid speaking and struggle with ideas during discussions.

Secondary Users: EAP Teachers
Lecturers who need better insights into student participation.

02 Needs

User Requirements

User Journey Map

Students feel anxious → avoid discussion → struggle for ideas → lack feedback → lose confidence.

3 Must-Have "Playful" Requirements

  • Low-pressure, judgment-free practice environment
  • User-controlled, gentle AI hints during silence
  • Supportive, clear feedback after discussion

Evidence of Life

We conducted user interviews and field observations to understand real student pain points.

Design Alternatives Comparison

Option 1: Automatic hints – Too intrusive, rejected.
Option 2: No hints – No support during struggles, rejected.
Option 3: User-controlled hints – Balanced support and independence, chosen.

05 Reflect

Evaluation & Reflection

Usability Testing

Testing with real EAP students showed improved confidence and reduced anxiety.

Iterative Refinement

We improved hint control and simplified feedback reports based on user feedback.

Final Reflection

XJTAIK puts students first, using AI to support—not replace—human learning. The design focuses on safety, respect, and confidence building.
